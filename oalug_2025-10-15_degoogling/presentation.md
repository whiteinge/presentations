## What is De-Googling?

- <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeGoogle>
- <https://www.reddit.com/r/degoogle/>

--
- Stop "paying" for their product.
- Cooperation with intelligence & law enforcement agencies:
  - Protest the practice.
  - Avoid data subpoenas if you are a target.
- Avoid data collection:
  - Targeted advertising.
  - Online profile.
- Web tracking across non-Google sites.
  - DoubleClick ads.
  - Like/Follow/Share buttons.
  - Google Analytics.
- Collection, use, and sharing of user data:
  - Across Google products,
- Privacy issues in its data collection and data retention policies.
